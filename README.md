### About the Experiment

This experiment identifies free amino acids and determines their concentration using thin-layer chromatography.
Silica or alumina coated as a thin layer on a glass or aluminium plate acts as the stationary phase, while the sample migrates upward by diffusion along with the mobile phase.
Separation depends on the functional groups present on the analyte, the composition of the mobile phase, and the nature and thickness of the stationary phase.
Amino acids are identified by comparing their Rf values, calculated as the distance travelled by the analyte relative to the distance travelled by the solvent front.
